<h1 align="center">fmbotg</h1>
<h3 align="center">
   A source-available Last.fm Telegram bot.
</h3>
<p align="center">
fmbotg is a social Telegram bot that provides music statistics for you, your friends, and your fellow group members.
</p>
<p align="center">
Easily see who listens to your favorite artist in a group and make new friends. Or discover new artists within your favorite genres.
</p>

<hr />

Send `/help` to the bot to see every command, or `/help <command>` for one of them.

Want to run your own copy or contribute? See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for setup, API keys and the full command list.

<h3>Based on .fmbot</h3>

fmbotg is a Python port of [.fmbot](https://github.com/fmbot-discord/fmbot), the Last.fm Discord bot, rebuilt for
Telegram with [aiogram](https://docs.aiogram.dev). It is released under the same license: see [LICENSE.MD](LICENSE.MD).
